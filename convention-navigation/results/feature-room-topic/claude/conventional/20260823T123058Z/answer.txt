**Rejecting over-length topics** — `update!`/`create!` would have raised into a 500. Both `Rooms::OpensController` and `Rooms::ClosedsController` now rescue `RecordInvalid`, re-render the form with what was typed still in it, put the error in the existing flash mechanism, and return 422. Nothing is saved: the rename doesn't go through, membership revisions don't run, and no sidebar broadcast fires. The input also carries `maxlength=120` as a client-side guard.

**Display** — the topic renders as a small second line under the room name in the room header (`rooms/show/_nav.html.erb`), plus a `font-weight: 400` rule so it reads as subordinate to the name. A translation entry `room_topic` was added for parity with `room_name`, since the two fields now sit side by side.
